Frequently Asked Questions

Why does per-word pricing hurt B2B copywriters?

Strategic research, positioning, and conversion architecture take the majority of hours—not typing words. Quote by deliverable outcome backed by buffered hours.

How should copywriters handle extra rewrite rounds?

Include two consolidated feedback rounds within 5 business days; additional stakeholder rounds trigger a pre-agreed change-order rate.

What utilization is realistic for senior B2B writers?

Around 55%–62%, because interviewing, deep research, pitching, and editing consume significant non-billable blocks.
